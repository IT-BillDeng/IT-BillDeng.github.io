AD-Reasoning introduces guideline-guided multimodal reasoning for Alzheimer's disease diagnosis. The work builds on AD-MultiSense, a 10,378-visit diagnostic question-answering dataset integrating structural MRI with six clinical modalities and guideline-validated rationales. The model combines bidirectional cross-attention fusion, LoRA tuning, and GRPO verifiable rewards, reaching 93.33% and 92.82% accuracy on CN-vs-CI and CN-vs-MCI, respectively.
